# Icon research

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser to see every option explored on 2026-11-07, round by round.

## Final choice

**A1 in indigo**: a white droplet holding a lightning bolt, on an indigo tile. The tile and bolt are the same indigo (`#4f46e5`), so the bolt reads as a cut-out of the drop. It means "electrolytes in water", has no text, and looks nothing like LMNT's wordmark (D4).

The app icon itself is [public/favicon.svg](../../public/favicon.svg). The PNGs are generated from it with `npm run icons`.

## How we got there

1. **Round 1, ten concepts:** droplet + crystal, vial, three ions, salt cube, charged drop, scoop, crystal + wave, hex lattice, shaker bottle, citrus drop. Matt liked **1 (droplet + crystal)** and **5 (charged drop)**.
2. **Round 2:** five shape variations of each, plus both in teal, ocean, lime, watermelon and midnight. Matt liked the **watermelon** color.
3. **Round 3:** watermelon as the background, the drop, or the bolt, paired with rind green, seed black, cream, mint and navy. Matt chose **A1**: watermelon tile and bolt with a white drop.
4. **Round 4:** A1 in 25 colors across reds, lemons, greens, blues and purples. Matt chose **indigo**.

## Notes for later

- Light tiles (Lemon, Lime zest, Sky, Lavender, Coral) make the white drop hard to see. If a lemon version is ever wanted, use Deep lemon (`#eab308`) or Honey (`#f59e0b`), or a dark drop.
- Thin outlines (1B, 5C) and small details (1D) blur below about 60 px.
- Very dark tiles (Crimson, Forest, Navy, Grape) can disappear on a dark wallpaper.
- Shortlist if indigo is ever revisited: Watermelon `#f43f5e`, Raspberry `#db2777`, Emerald `#059669`, Royal `#2563eb`, Violet `#7c3aed`.
